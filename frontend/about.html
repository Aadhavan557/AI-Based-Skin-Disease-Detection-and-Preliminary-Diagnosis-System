🔬 Our Mission

What is DermaAI?

DermaAI is an AI-powered preliminary skin analysis platform designed to help users better understand their skin health. By leveraging advanced deep learning and computer vision, we provide fast, accessible, and preliminary insights into visible skin conditions.

Our platform is built as an academic and educational tool — a demonstration of how modern AI can assist in healthcare awareness, while always emphasizing the irreplaceable role of qualified medical professionals.

DermaAI does not provide medical diagnoses. Every result is clearly marked as preliminary and for educational purposes only.

⚙️ Technology

How the AI Works

Our model uses state-of-the-art deep learning techniques for visual skin pattern recognition.

🖼️ Computer Vision

The model processes the uploaded image as a multi-dimensional tensor of pixel values. It identifies spatial patterns, textures, colors, and structural features in the skin image.

🧬 Convolutional Neural Network

A deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) extracts hierarchical visual features — from edges and textures in early layers to complex skin patterns in deeper layers.

🔄 Transfer Learning

The model uses transfer learning from established architectures pre-trained on ImageNet, then fine-tuned on a dermatological dataset to specialize in skin condition recognition.

📊 Confidence Scoring

The model's final layer uses Softmax activation to output probability distributions across all classes. The highest probability becomes the predicted class with an associated confidence score.

🗂️ Training Dataset

The model was trained on a curated dataset of labeled dermatological images across 6 classes. Data augmentation techniques were applied to improve generalization and robustness.

🌡️ GradCAM Visualization

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) highlights the regions of the image that most influenced the model's prediction, providing visual explainability.

⚠️ Limitations

Important Limitations

🤖
AI is Not a Doctor

DermaAI uses pattern recognition, not medical judgment. It cannot account for patient history, symptoms, duration, or physical examination findings that a trained dermatologist would assess.

📷
Image Quality Matters

The quality of the uploaded image significantly affects prediction accuracy. Blurry, poorly lit, or obstructed images may lead to incorrect or low-confidence predictions.

🎯
Limited to Trained Classes

The model can only identify conditions it was trained on (Acne, Eczema, Psoriasis, Melanoma, Ringworm, Healthy Skin). Other conditions may be misclassified.

🏥
Always Seek Professional Care

No AI prediction should delay or replace seeking professional medical care. For any serious, persistent, or worsening skin condition, always consult a qualified dermatologist.
